# Step 2: decisions log

Decisions taken while reviewing the schema. All decisions below have been applied to the schema, the checker, the sample content, the tests and the schema map.

| # | Issue | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Degree requirements | One set of requirements per curriculum (programme and year). They move inside the curriculum file as a `requirements` section: total ECTS, minimum semesters, rules. The separate `degree_requirements.json` and its older-version folders go away. | decided |
| 2 | Names of people and partners not translatable | Names of faculty, administration people and partner universities become text per language (`en` and/or `el`). A person's name is written once, in `faculty.json`, and every other file points at the person by id. An administration role has either a `faculty_id` or its own translated name, never both. | decided |
| 3 | Two groupings: category and school | Drop the category (`categories.json` and `department.category` are removed). Keep the school as the only grouping, and keep it optional. The hierarchy is university, school, department, programme. | decided |
| 4 | Sharing across departments (faculty, courses, prerequisites) | References may point across departments and programmes: a course's teachers, a course's prerequisites, a programme director and an administration role. A reference to another department is written with the department first, e.g. `mathematics:euler-leo` or `cs:3117`. A person or course is still written once, in its home department. A course in another programme of the same department is written `cs/msc-data-science:DS501`, only when the code alone would be ambiguous. | decided |
| 5 | List of universities and departments | An index file `content/index.json` lists the universities in the order to show. Each university's `university.json` lists its departments in order. Anything not listed is not shown, and the checker warns when a folder exists but is not listed. | decided |
| 6 | Information stored twice | (a) Head of department name: settled by decision 2. (b) Levels and cycles stay inside each curriculum file, so an old year always shows what applied then; the editor guide says to copy last year's file as the start of a new one. (c) A free elective points at a course (`mathematics:1001`) and takes name and ECTS from there. For a course not in any curriculum on the site it carries its own details instead, never both. (d) `instructor_names` stays for people not in any faculty list, written as translatable names. | decided |
| 7 | Checks that are missing | A course gets one optional value, `available_from` (whole number 1 to 12): the earliest study semester a student may take it. `semesters` stays as where the course is taught. Without `available_from` the earliest is the course's first semester. Checks: `available_from` is not later than the course's first semester; no odd or even enforcement on `semesters`; a curriculum year with no calendar is a warning that does not block; a course's own `language` accepts only `en` or `el`. | decided |
| 8 | Fixed English lists (ranks, degree types, holiday kinds) and site translations | The fixed lists stay in the data. The Greek and English labels for each value go in one site-level file, to be created in step 6. A faculty member with rank `other` can add an optional `other_title`, a title written per language (for example "Reader"). | decided |
| 9 | Course details (hours, website, type) and contact or logo | No extra course fields: at Greek universities these are the same for all courses, so they are not stored per course. A faculty member gets an optional `meeting_hours`, written per language (for example "Mondays 10:00 to 12:00"). Logo and university contact details are left out for now. | decided |
